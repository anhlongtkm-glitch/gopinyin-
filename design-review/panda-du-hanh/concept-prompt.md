# Panda Du Hành — Gameplay concept prompt

Tool: built-in image_gen. Canonical reference: assets/mascot/welcome.webp.
Status: concept illustration, not implemented UI or production sprite.

Use case: ui-mockup / identity-preserve.
Create a polished landscape 3:2 visual concept of a completely redesigned educational space typing arcade game called "Panda Du Hành" for GoPinyin. This is a visual proposal for a real game, not a promotional poster.

Reference image role: canonical GoPinyin panda identity. Preserve this exact friendly rounded panda face, ivory-white and emerald-teal fluffy fur, big emerald eyes, dark teal nose, and teal scarf. Transform its standing pose into a seated spaceship pilot. Do not use a black-and-white panda.

Main composition: one coherent high fidelity desktop gameplay screen, front-on, with no laptop/device frame or busy surrounding art board. Large calm central playfield. Main game title at upper left "Panda Du Hành". A compact top HUD has topic "Gia đình", timer "01:12", score "2.460", and a shield icon "3/3"; a small second line "Combo ×2". Deep midnight indigo/teal space with subtle nebula at the edges and sparse stars, low detail behind the words. A distant warm little family-house planet is tucked at the upper left edge, another small moon near the upper right. Not a rainbow space explosion.

The signature hero is near the bottom center: the canonical fluffy emerald panda visibly SITTING INSIDE a beautifully designed rounded compact ivory and emerald spacecraft, both paws holding cockpit controls, lower body behind the dashboard, entire face/ears/scarf clearly visible through a clear glass dome with subtle reflections. The spaceship has short side wings, two small mint-energy engines, a small articulated energy emitter that can aim toward a target while the panda stays upright. The hero occupies around 17 percent of screen height and is readable, with enough calm room above for typing targets. It must look physically seated in a designed cockpit, not a pasted standing mascot. Premium soft 3D stylized rendering, consistent gentle light. No helmet covering the panda's ears or face.

Targets: exactly four floating readable data capsules, widely separated in the central 60 percent of the playfield. Rounded matte dark navy panels with restrained 3D ivory/teal frames, large crisp Chinese characters and a small Vietnamese meaning. Use exact pairs: "爸爸" with "Bố", "妈妈" with "Mẹ", "哥哥" with "Anh trai", "妹妹" with "Em gái". One target is clearly selected by a thin mint targeting bracket. One target has a small gold star badge and "Thưởng". No complete Pinyin answers because this is competitive mode. A tiny mint energy projectile with a white core and short graceful tail travels from the ship emitter toward the selected capsule. No gunfire obscuring Chinese text. Near the bottom edges, compact functional pill controls "Tập trung" and "Tùy chọn". Three small gold/teal shield elements near ship may suggest defense, but do not duplicate a second HUD.

Art direction: premium soft 3D mascot and spacecraft combined with clean, restrained game UI; ivory/emerald hero, indigo space, mint energy, warm gold reward, subtle coral warning only. Comfortable rounded forms and clear typographic hierarchy. Vietnamese words and title must be spelled exactly with accents. Avoid tiny dense copy, sidebars, cards containing paragraphs, giant glow, excessive glassmorphism, fake browser frames, watermark, other logos, extra mascot, extra limbs or duplicate spacecraft. Game text is mockup illustration only; design should feel implementable and readable at a smaller size.
